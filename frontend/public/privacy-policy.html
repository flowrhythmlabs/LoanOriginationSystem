Privacy Policy

This draft privacy policy is intended as a starting point for Bluewaves Distributors Private Limited (BDPL) and should be reviewed and approved by the business before production launch.

Information we collect

When a user submits an inquiry through the BDPL website, we may collect the information provided in the form, including name, business email, mobile number, company name, designation, inquiry type, subject, and message.

Purpose of collection

This information is collected to respond to business partnership, distributor, merchant, telecom, digital services, or brand-related inquiries. It may be reviewed by the appropriate BDPL contact or team for follow-up discussions.

How we use the information

Submitted inquiry data may be used to assess the request, contact the sender, and manage business communication related to the inquiry. The business should confirm the specific internal workflow, contact owners, and retention rules before launch.

Sharing

Submission data may be shared only with the personnel or service providers responsible for reviewing business inquiries. The business should confirm any external service usage, notification delivery method, and data-sharing boundaries before production.

Retention

The data retention period should be confirmed by BDPL before production. A reasonable business-inquiry retention period may be used, but the exact policy should be approved by the business and, where appropriate, legal or compliance teams.

Your rights and contact

Users may contact BDPL to ask questions about their inquiry information, request access to the information they submitted, or seek correction or deletion where applicable. The final contact details and process should be confirmed before launch.

Important confirmation needed

BDPL should confirm the final privacy policy language, the responsible team for inquiry review, any required retention period, and whether external data processors are involved before the site goes live.
