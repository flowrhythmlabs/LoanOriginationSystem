Privacy Notice

This page is a draft privacy notice for the FinEdge Loan Origination System prototype. Personal, identity, employment, income, banking and document information is collected to process and assess a loan application and to meet applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

KYC, credit bureau and banking checks should only be initiated with the relevant consent. Access should be limited to authorised staff, and sensitive identifiers should be masked wherever appropriate.

Before production launch, the NBFC/legal/compliance team must approve and replace this draft with the complete privacy notice, data retention rules, grievance contact details and applicable regulatory disclosures.
